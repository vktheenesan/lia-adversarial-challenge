## Reporting a Vulnerability or Invariant Bypass

The Librae AI Labs security engineering team takes all reports of potential security vulnerabilities, invariant breaches, memory drift, or adversarial bypasses seriously.

If you believe you have discovered:
1. A payload that circumvents LIA's invariant policy without detection; OR
2. An unhandled exception, denial of service, or hypervisor panic; OR
3. Measurable memory leakage (> 0 bytes) over continuous high-throughput execution:

### Reporting Channels

## Response Timeline & SLA

* **Initial Acknowledgment:** Within 24 hours.
* **Reproduction & Verification:** Within 48 hours.
* **Triage & Remediation Plan:** Within 5 business days.

Valid findings will receive formal public credit in our advisory hall of fame and coordination for CVE assignment if applicable.
